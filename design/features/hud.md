# HUD

Status: draft (built overnight, not yet reviewed)

* **Goal:** always know your coins, what you're carrying, and the next thing to do.
* **Layout:** coins pill (top center) · goal banner under it · save-status chip (only when not saving) · toasts under that · basket (top left) · UPGRADES / RECIPES / SOUND buttons (right middle).
* **States:** loading ("Loading your shop..."), market full, normal, shelf full (potion count turns red), test mode (orange chip).
* **Badges:** red "!" on UPGRADES when something is affordable; red "NEW" on RECIPES when an unlocked recipe hasn't been brewed yet.
* **Mobile:** nothing in the bottom corners; buttons at least 52 px tall (design pixels).
